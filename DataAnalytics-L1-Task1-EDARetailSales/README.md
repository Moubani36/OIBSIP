📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship.
The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, product performance, and actionable business insights using Python.
The analysis includes data cleaning, descriptive statistics, trend analysis, customer demographics, correlation analysis, and business recommendations supported by data visualizations.

🎯 Objectives

- Inspect and understand the dataset
- Handle missing values
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Study customer demographics
- Evaluate product category performance
- Identify relationships between numerical variables
- Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
- GitHub

📂 Dataset Information

- Dataset: Retail Sales Dataset
- Records: 2,000
- Cleaned Records: 1,987
- Features: Customer demographics, product category, quantity, pricing, sales, and transaction details.

> Note: The dataset contains product **categories** but does not include individual product names. Therefore, the product analysis focuses on category-level performance.

📊 Key Insights

- 📈 Sales increase significantly during **Q4 (October–December)**, indicating strong seasonal demand.
- 👥 Male and female customers contribute almost equally to overall sales.
- 💻 Electronics generates the highest revenue among all product categories.
- 💰 Products with higher unit prices contribute significantly to total sales.
- 📊 Profitability differs across product categories and customer gender, creating opportunities for targeted marketing.

💡 Business Recommendations

1️⃣ Prepare for Seasonal Demand
Increase inventory and marketing campaigns before Q4 to maximize revenue during peak shopping periods.

2️⃣ Target Marketing by Category
Promote Clothing products to female customers while emphasizing premium Electronics and Beauty products to male customers.

3️⃣ Increase Average Order Value
Encourage customers to purchase higher-priced products through bundled offers, premium recommendations, and personalized promotions.

4️⃣ Improve Beauty Category Performance
Introduce discounts, loyalty rewards, and cross-selling strategies to increase sales in the Beauty category.

5️⃣ Leverage Customer Insights
Continue analyzing customer demographics and purchasing behavior to create more personalized marketing campaigns.
